Fictional café · Riverside

Small cups, big cinnamon energy.

Maple & Moth is a make-believe coffee shop on Lantern Lane. We steep tea slowly, pull espresso loudly, and bake scones before the sun finds the windows. Come for the cardamom knots; stay for the window seat.
